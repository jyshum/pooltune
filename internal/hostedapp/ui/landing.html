How it works

From slow query
to verified repair.

01

Observe the workload

Pooltune watches the queries your application already runs and finds lookups worth investigating.

02

Find the cause

The agent checks the execution plan and table statistics to explain why a lookup slowed down.

03

Repair and verify

With your permission, Pooltune applies an eligible repair and compares the same sampled input before and after.
